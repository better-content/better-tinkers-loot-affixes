## Primary implemented gameplay promise

Player-attributed kills of eligible hostile mobs and `chests/*` loot tables can yield affixed Tinkers Construct parts, caches and reforging currencies (`GlobalAffixLoot`, `AffixItems`). The cache yields one material-compatible affixed part on successful server-side use, with natural-origin/provenance/salvage metadata (`TConAffixRewards`, `AffixCrafting`). Parts' compatible affixes transfer into assembled TConstruct tools with stat/modifier effects (`TConAffixRewards`, `AffixModifiers`); player-inventory interaction arms currency by right-click and applies it to a part by left-click, with confirmation for ruinous mutation and shift-right-click salvage (`AffixClientEvents`, `AffixNetwork`). The server checks menu, slot, current stack fingerprint and per-player nonce; successful operations consume one currency, while successful salvage spends the part's payout once. This is **not** proof of end-to-end crafting GUI behavior on a real client.

Acquisition references: `better_tinkers_loot_affixes:affixed_part_cache`, `recasting_flux`, `grafting_flux`, `shearing_flux`, `forerune_seal`, `afterrune_seal`, `ruinous_flux` are registered in `AffixItems.kt`; `GlobalAffixLoot.kt` adds cache/currency pools to `chests/*` loot tables and rolls parts/currency for eligible player-attributed hostile-mob kills, excluding spawner-origin mobs and the Bumblezone/Ratlantis dimensions. Default outside-Font chances in `TConAffixConfig.kt` are 1% part, 1% currency, 3% chest cache, 3% chest currency; Nether/Aether Font kills use 3% part and 4% currency. `AffixOrigins.kt` maps the Nether and Aether dimensions to themed part/material/affix pools (optional exclusive part pools fall back); other dimensions use global. Natural part salvage can yield currency, but ordinary crafting recipes for these currencies or a new block are **not** registered here. TConstruct parts are selected from `TConAffixRewards.allPartProfiles`; optional parts/materials require their actual provider mods. The relevant IDs and target item acquisition should be verified in the assembled pack, not inferred from source alone.
